10 Best Tools to Automate LinkedIn Drip Campaigns in 2025 - Monday, 17 November 2025

To succeed at LinkedIn prospecting, you need more than just a single connection request. The most effective outreach strategies utilize drip campaigns—automated sequences of timed touchpoints that guide prospects through your sales funnel with minimal effort.

LinkedIn drip campaign automation enables you to build multi-step sequences that combine connection requests, follow-up messages, and strategic delays. This method can boost response rates by 300-500% compared to single-message outreach, saving you hours of manual work.

This guide explores the 10 best tools for automating LinkedIn drip campaigns in 2025, comparing their features, pricing, and ease of use to help you choose the right solution.

Disclaimer: LinkedIn's Terms of Service restrict automation. Use these tools responsibly with personalized messages, realistic delays (5-7 days between messages), and safety features to protect your account.

1. Bindago

Website: https://bindago.com/

A comprehensive desktop application for LinkedIn automation that excels at creating and running multi-step drip campaigns. It allows you to set up connection request sequences with automatic follow-ups, conditional message flows, and intelligent timing from an intuitive interface.

Pricing

$9.99/month (flat rate, all features included)

Drip Campaign Features:

  • Unlimited multi-step message sequences
  • Automatic follow-ups based on connection acceptance
  • Message templates with dynamic personalization
  • Smart delays between sequence steps
  • Campaign pause/resume functionality

Best For: Professionals, small businesses, and sales teams seeking an affordable, secure, all-in-one drip campaign solution.

2. Dripify

Website: https://dripify.com/

A cloud-based platform specializing in LinkedIn drip campaigns with advanced sequence logic, automated follow-ups, and comprehensive analytics.

Pricing

Starts at $39/month for the basic plan.

Best For: Users who prioritize detailed analytics and are willing to pay a premium for specialized drip campaign features.

3. Expandi.io

Website: https://expandi.io/

A full-featured sequence automation tool with connection requests, follow-ups, messaging drip campaigns, and AI personalization.

Pricing

$99/month per user for standard plans.

Best For: Agencies and teams needing sophisticated multi-channel drip campaigns with AI capabilities.

4. LinkedHelper

Website: https://linkedhelper.com/

A desktop and extension-based tool allowing for campaign sequences with custom follow-ups, precise delays, and reply detection.

Pricing

  • Standard: $15/month
  • PRO: $45/month

Best For: Power users who want deep customization and don't mind a steeper learning curve.

5. Waalaxy

Website: https://waalaxy.com/

A visual prospecting automation platform with LinkedIn invitation sequences, message flows, and campaign workflows through a drag-and-drop interface.

Pricing

Freemium (80 invites/month); paid plans from €19/month.

Best For: Users who prefer a visual, drag-and-drop interface for building sequences.

6. Meet Alfred

Website: https://meetalfred.com/

A multi-channel sequence platform combining LinkedIn, email, and Twitter automation with coordinated drip campaigns.

Pricing

$29-59/month for individual users.

Best For: Users needing synchronized drip campaigns across multiple platforms.

7. Octopus CRM

Website: https://octopuscrm.io/

A Chrome extension with workflow campaigns including connection requests, message sequences, and follow-ups through a simple funnel-based approach.

Pricing

  • Starter: $9.99/month
  • Pro: $14.99/month

Best For: Budget-conscious users seeking simple sequence automation.

8. PhantomBuster

Website: https://phantombuster.com/

A workflow automation platform with "Phantoms" (pre-built automations) to run multi-step outreach sequences.

Pricing

Paid plans starting at €56/month for sequence-capable plans.

Best For: Technical users who need to combine LinkedIn sequences with other automation workflows.

9. Salesflow

Website: https://salesflow.com/

A cloud-based outreach automation platform for managing campaigns with multiple sequence steps, designed with agencies in mind.

Pricing

$99/month based on publicly available comparisons.

Best For: Agencies needing team collaboration features for managing multiple client campaigns.

10. LinkedFusion

Website: https://www.linkedfusion.io/

A sequence automation platform supporting both LinkedIn and email steps in coordinated campaigns.

Pricing

  • Professional: $65.95/user/month
  • Grow: $95.95/user/month

Best For: Users wanting tightly integrated LinkedIn and email drip sequences.

Conclusion

After a comprehensive evaluation, Bindago stands out as the best overall LinkedIn drip campaign tool for most users. It offers exceptional value at $9.99/month, advanced sequencing capabilities, and maximum security as a desktop app.
