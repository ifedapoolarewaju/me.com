A Guide to the Top 10 Tools for Exporting LinkedIn Leads to CSV in 2025

LinkedIn Sales Navigator is a powerhouse for identifying and qualifying leads. However, getting that valuable data out of LinkedIn and into your CRM, email marketing software, or even a simple spreadsheet can be a major hurdle. Since LinkedIn lacks a native bulk export feature for most users, a critical gap exists in many sales and marketing workflows.

This is where lead export tools come in. These solutions are designed to extract lead data from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator, providing you with CSV or Excel files filled with names, job titles, company details, and sometimes even enriched contact information like email addresses. In this guide, we'll review the 10 best tools for this job in 2025, comparing their features, pricing, and overall value.

Disclaimer: Many of these tools use scraping techniques that may be against LinkedIn's Terms of Service. It's essential to use them responsibly, adhere to data privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA, and export leads in smaller, manageable batches to minimize any risk to your account.

1. Bindago

  • Website: https://bindago.com/
  • What it is: A desktop application that exports lead lists from both LinkedIn searches and Sales Navigator to CSV/Excel. It captures all available profile data and also includes a full suite of outreach automation tools.
  • Price: $9.99/month (flat rate)
  • Key Features:
    • Export Sales Navigator and LinkedIn search results to CSV.
    • Includes all public profile fields in the export.
    • Also offers bulk connection requests, mass InMails, and message sequences.
    • Runs locally for maximum security.
  • Platform: Desktop App (Windows/Mac/Linux)
  • Best For: Professionals and small businesses looking for a secure, affordable, all-in-one solution for both lead export and outreach.

Why Bindago is a Top Choice: Bindago offers the best value in this category. For a low monthly fee, you get a complete LinkedIn automation toolkit, not just a CSV exporter. As a desktop app, it keeps your LinkedIn credentials secure on your machine. It's a comprehensive solution for anyone who wants to find, export, and engage with leads all in one place.

2. Evaboot

  • Website: https://evaboot.com/
  • What it is: A Chrome extension focused on extracting clean, high-quality leads from Sales Navigator, with features for data cleaning and email enrichment.
  • Price: Starts at €29/month for ~2,000 lead extractions.
  • Best For: Users who prioritize data quality and need enriched email addresses with their exports.

3. OutX.ai

  • Website: https://outx.ai/
  • What it is: A Sales Navigator scraper that also finds and verifies email addresses, cleans data, and exports both lead and account lists.
  • Price: $99/month (for certain credit tiers).
  • Best For: Users who need verified email addresses and are willing to pay a premium for high-quality data.

4. PhantomBuster

  • Website: https://phantombuster.com/
  • What it is: A powerful workflow automation platform with "Phantoms" that can extract leads from Sales Navigator lists, company pages, and regular LinkedIn searches.
  • Price: Starts around $69/month depending on usage.
  • Best For: Tech-savvy users who want to build complex automation workflows that combine scraping with other actions.

5. Surfe

  • Website: https://surfe.com/
  • What it is: A tool that exports Sales Navigator lead lists to CSV or syncs them directly with your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.).
  • Price: Varies; a free trial is available.
  • Best For: Teams that rely on major CRM platforms and want seamless integration.

6. SalesXporter

  • Website: https://salesxporter.com/
  • What it is: A Chrome extension for exporting leads from Sales Navigator to CSV or Excel, with optional email enrichment.
  • Price: Tiered pricing from $27/month for 2,000 leads.
  • Best For: Users with predictable monthly export needs who want straightforward, volume-based pricing.

7. NioMaker

  • Website: https://linkedin-sales-nav-export.niomaker.com/
  • What it is: A tool for exporting people and companies from Sales Navigator, with data cleaning and email enrichment in its PRO plan.
  • Price: Free plan available; PRO plan is $19.99/month.
  • Best For: Budget-conscious users who need occasional exports and appreciate a generous free tier.

8. NioLeads

  • Website: https://nioleads.com/
  • What it is: A Chrome extension with a dashboard for exporting Sales Navigator leads to CSV, with optional email enrichment.
  • Price: Not public; requires a demo.
  • Best For: Users who prefer a dashboard interface for managing their exports.

9. NavLead

  • Website: https://navlead.com/
  • What it is: A credit-based system for exporting leads and finding email addresses, with data cleaning filters.
  • Price: Credit-based; pay as you go.
  • Best For: Users with fluctuating monthly needs who prefer a flexible pricing model.

10. Wiza

  • Website: https://wiza.co/
  • What it is: A tool that extracts lead lists from Sales Navigator, verifies emails, and exports clean CSV files optimized for cold emailing.
  • Price: Credit-based with pay-as-you-go options.
  • Best For: Cold email marketers who need verified emails with high deliverability scores.

Final Thoughts

Exporting LinkedIn leads is a vital step for any modern sales or recruiting team. While LinkedIn doesn't offer this feature natively, a variety of third-party tools have emerged to fill the void.

When choosing a tool, it's important to balance affordability, security, features, and ease of use. Bindago stands out as the top choice for most users, offering a complete automation toolkit for just $9.99/month, all while keeping your data secure on your desktop.
