A Renewed Guide to the 10 Best LinkedIn Tools for Mass Connection Requests in 2025

Scaling up your LinkedIn connection requests can significantly broaden your professional circle, uncover fresh leads, and open doors to new business ventures. Manually sending out connection requests one by one, however, is a drain on your time and energy, particularly when your target list includes hundreds or even thousands of prospects.

LinkedIn automation tools address this challenge by allowing you to dispatch connection requests in bulk, complete with personalized messages, automated follow-ups, and intelligent scheduling. In this guide, we will delve into the 10 best LinkedIn tools for mass connection requests in 2025, evaluating their features, cost, and unique advantages to help you select the ideal solution for your outreach efforts.

Disclaimer: It is crucial to use LinkedIn automation tools with care. Tailor your messages, adhere to LinkedIn's daily activity limits, incorporate natural delays, and focus on relevant prospects to keep your account secure and improve your response rates.

1. Bindago

  • Website: https://bindago.com/
  • Overview: A desktop application for LinkedIn automation that excels at sending bulk connection requests, mass InMail campaigns, multi-step message sequences, and bulk invitation withdrawals. It operates locally on your machine for enhanced security.
  • Price: $9.99/month (flat rate)
  • Key Features:
    • Bulk connection requests with personalized notes
    • Multi-step messaging sequences
    • Mass InMail sending to Sales Navigator leads
    • Bulk withdrawal of pending invitations
    • Export LinkedIn search results to CSV
  • Platform: Desktop App (Windows/Mac/Linux)
  • Best For: Professionals and small businesses looking for an affordable, secure, and easy-to-use automation tool.

Why Bindago is a Top Choice: Unlike its cloud-based counterparts, Bindago runs directly on your computer, ensuring your LinkedIn credentials are never stored on a third-party server. This provides a higher level of security and privacy. At just $9.99/month, it is the most budget-friendly option on this list while still providing all the essential automation features.

2. Octopus CRM

  • Website: https://octopuscrm.io/
  • Overview: A Chrome extension that automates connection requests, personalized messages, and follow-ups, with integrations for various CRMs.
  • Price:
    • Starter: $9.99/month
    • Pro: $14.99/month
    • Advanced: $21.99/month
    • Unlimited: $39.99/month
  • Best For: Users looking for a low-cost Chrome extension with CRM integration.

3. LinkedHelper 2

  • Website: https://linkedhelper.com/
  • Overview: A desktop tool that sends bulk connection requests, manages follow-ups, and offers extensive campaign management features.
  • Price:
    • Standard: $15/month
    • Pro: $45/month
  • Best For: Users who need a desktop-based solution with powerful customization options.

4. Expandi.io

  • Website: https://expandi.io/
  • Overview: A cloud-based platform that uses smart sequences and AI-powered personalization for multi-channel outreach.
  • Price: Starts at $99/month per user.
  • Best For: Agencies and teams that require a premium cloud solution with advanced features.

5. Dux-Soup

  • Website: https://www.dux-soup.com/
  • Overview: A lightweight Chrome extension for bulk connection invitations, profile visits, and simple follow-ups.
  • Price: Paid plans start at €12.99/month per seat.
  • Best For: Users who want a simple and straightforward Chrome extension for basic outreach.

6. PhantomBuster

  • Website: https://phantombuster.com/
  • Overview: A flexible automation tool that can scrape profiles, send connection invites, and automate messages.
  • Price: Plans start around €56/month.
  • Best For: Tech-savvy users who need to combine data scraping with outreach automation.

7. Dripify

  • Website: https://dripify.com/
  • Overview: A specialized tool for creating LinkedIn drip campaigns with automated follow-ups and lead management.
  • Price: Starts at $39/user/month.
  • Best For: Users focused on creating sophisticated drip campaigns with detailed analytics.

8. Meet Alfred

  • Website: https://meetalfred.com/
  • Overview: A multi-channel outreach platform that includes LinkedIn, email, and Twitter automation.
  • Price: Paid plans from $29/month per user.
  • Best For: Users who need to coordinate outreach across multiple platforms.

9. Waalaxy

  • Website: https://waalaxy.com/
  • Overview: A tool focused on LinkedIn prospecting with a visual sequence builder and email integration.
  • Price: Starts around €56/month for basic outreach.
  • Best For: Users who prefer a visual campaign builder and multi-channel capabilities.

10. Zopto

  • Website: https://zopto.com/
  • Overview: A cloud-based platform for high-volume outreach with advanced filtering and CRM integrations.
  • Price: Starts at ~$215/month.
  • Best For: Large organizations and agencies with substantial budgets.

Final Thoughts

Automating your LinkedIn connection requests can significantly boost your networking and lead generation efforts. For most users who need a balance of affordability, security, and functionality, Bindago is the top choice. At just $9.99/month, it offers a complete and secure solution for scaling your outreach.
